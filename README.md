# GrandStay Hotel Management System

GrandStay is a modern hotel management solution designed to simplify and organize everyday hotel operations in one place.

## Live Demo

Experience the GrandStay Hotel Management System live:

🔗 https://gunasri-km.github.io/GrandStay-Hotel-Management-System/

## Overview

The system provides a centralized platform for managing hotel rooms, guests, reservations, dining services, billing, and daily availability. It helps organize important hotel activities and provides a smooth management experience.

## Key Features

- Dashboard for daily hotel overview
- Guest management
- Room availability and status management
- Booking and reservation management
- Check-in and check-out management
- Daily food and menu availability
- Billing and revenue management
- Date-wise hotel operations
- Search and organized data management
- Responsive and user-friendly interface

## Main Modules

### 1. Dashboard
Provides a quick overview of rooms, guests, bookings, food services, and daily activities.

### 2. Guest Management
Allows hotel staff to add, view, search, and manage guest information.

### 3. Room Management
Displays room availability and room status such as available, occupied, cleaning, and maintenance.

### 4. Booking Management
Helps manage reservations, check-ins, check-outs, and booking status.

### 5. Food & Menu Management
Provides daily breakfast, lunch, and dinner menu availability and allows menu items to be managed.

### 6. Billing & Revenue
Organizes room billing and provides a clear view of hotel revenue for daily operations.

## Daily Management

GrandStay is designed around daily hotel operations. Selecting a particular date allows the system to display and manage the relevant rooms, bookings, and food availability for that day.
